ABOUT US

A site management partner built on ethics and compliance.

SyncVision Research Solutions is a Site Management Organisation (SMO) working in clinical research. We provide access to hospital networks for pharmaceutical sponsors and Contract Research Organisations (CROs).

Through our site tie-ups in Maharashtra and Bihar, we help sponsors reach diverse patient populations while delivering ethical, well-documented trial support at every site.

We also offer skill-based clinical research courses to help the next generation of professionals build careers in the field.

WHAT WE DO

Site support for sponsors and CROs.

Hospital Network Access

Ready access to hospital sites in Maharashtra and Bihar, for faster site identification and onboarding.

GCP-Compliant Conduct

Trial activities carried out in line with ICH-GCP and NDCT guidelines, with accurate and audit-ready documentation.

Ethical Trial Support

Patient safety, informed consent and ethics committee requirements handled with care at every site we manage.
